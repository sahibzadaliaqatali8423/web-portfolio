    Questions, answered

    Can’t find what you need? Our support team replies within one working day.

    Do my clients need a Ledgerline account?

    No. Clients get a secure link and pay by card or bank transfer in their own currency. They never need to sign up.

    How long until the money reaches my bank?

    Most payouts arrive in 1 to 2 working days after your client pays. Card payments can take one extra day the first time.

    What exchange rate do you use?

    The mid-market rate, the one you see on independent currency sites. The fee for your plan is the only charge, shown before you send.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes. Paid plans are month to month, or yearly with two months free. You keep access to every invoice you’ve sent.

    Which countries can I get paid in?

    Clients can pay from 34 currencies. Payouts are available to bank accounts in more than 70 countries.

    Is there a limit on invoice size?

    There’s no limit on invoice value. Very large payments may need a quick identity check the first time.
