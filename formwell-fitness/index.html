Good questions

Still unsure? Start the free trial. It takes a minute and you can cancel in two taps.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every workout works with bodyweight only. If you have dumbbells or a resistance band, Formwell can add them to your plan.

I’m a complete beginner. Is it too hard?

Beginner plans start with short sets and longer rests, and every move has an easier option. Your plan only gets harder when you’re ready.

How does the form coach use my camera?

The coach analyses movement on your phone. Video is never recorded or uploaded, and you can turn the coach off at any time.

Can I cancel my trial?

Yes. Cancel any time before the 7 days end and you won’t be charged.

Does it work with my smartwatch?

Formwell syncs workouts and heart rate with the health app on your phone, which most smartwatches already connect to.

Is Formwell medical advice?

No. Formwell is a fitness app. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a doctor before starting a new exercise plan.
