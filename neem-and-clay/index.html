How a piece is made

Four hands, three weeks, one cup.

We work with family workshops instead of factories. It is slower, and it is why no two glazes or wood grains come out quite the same.

i.

Sourcing

River clay, reclaimed oak and organic linen from within a day's drive of each workshop.

Days 1–3
ii.

Shaping

Vases are thrown on a kick wheel and frames are joined with hand-cut mortise and tenon.

Days 4–9
iii.

Firing & finishing

Stoneware is fired twice. Wood is oiled by hand in three coats, a day apart.

Days 10–18
iv.

Signing & packing

The maker signs the base, we log the batch number, and it ships in recycled paper with no plastic.

Days 19–21

Free delivery over $250

White-glove delivery for furniture, tracked courier for everything else.

30-day returns

Not right for the room? Send it back unused for a full refund.

Five-year guarantee

On every joint and frame we make. Ceramics are covered for breakage in transit.

Fair pay, published

Makers set their own price per piece and we publish the split each season.
